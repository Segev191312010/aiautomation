AUTOTRADER V1 - REAL-TIME IBKR PAPER AUTOMATION

PURPOSE
Build a local-only automated trader that reads real-time IBKR market data and
submits only IBKR PAPER orders. LIVE accounts, LIVE ports, and LIVE runtime
paths are forbidden.

LOCKED RUNTIME CONTRACT
- Engine native host: 127.0.0.1.
- TWS port: 7497 only.
- One configured PAPER (DU-prefixed) account and two dedicated client IDs:
  one for market data, one for orders. They must differ.
- Runtime and evidence accept only real IBKR live streaming data or real IBKR
  historical bars. Delayed, frozen, stale, incomplete, duplicate, malformed,
  or pacing-failed data blocks the session.
- Fixtures and fake brokers are test-only. Runtime and evidence import guards
  reject them.
- PAPER execution requires a signed receipt; real-data shadow does not.
- A PAPER receipt binds build SHA, evidence digest, strategy and policy hashes,
  PAPER account, data and order client IDs, owner approval, issue time, and
  expiry. Expiry is at most 30 days after issue.
- TWS auto-start requires a valid receipt, healthy preflight, clean
  reconciliation, a healthy scheduler, and no kill state.

FIRST ACTIVE STRATEGY: ORB_V1
- US common stocks only; long entries only; regular trading hours only.
- Opening range: 09:30 through 09:45 America/New_York.
- Scanner: HOT_BY_VOLUME at 09:45:05 ET, retaining all rankings and admitting
  at most 50 qualified symbols.
- First completed five-minute bar closing above the opening-range high may
  qualify once per symbol per session.
- Entry cutoff: 12:00 ET.
- Price: 10 through 300 USD; 20-day average dollar volume at least 20M USD.
- Fresh quote: at most two seconds old; spread at most 15 bps.
- Parent limit may chase no more than 20 bps beyond the breakout close.
- Stop: exactly one Wilder ATR(14) below entry. Target: exactly 2R.
- AI only returns PROPOSE or ABSTAIN for a completed RuleSetup. It cannot edit
  prices, quantity, account, authority, or broker commands.
- Risk alone sizes orders and enforces: maximum three positions, 0.5 percent
  net-liq risk per trade, 2 percent daily-loss breaker, notional limits, one
  trade per symbol/day, and no pyramiding.

DELIVERY TICKETS

RTP-00 BASELINE AND GOVERNANCE
- Preserve the existing dirty rebuild and unrelated worktrees.
- Do not modify .env files, SQLite databases, or paper review protocol docs.
- Configure a legitimate local Git identity before any commit.
- Record exact baseline SHA, full quality-gate output, and local archive.
- Keep work local until the owner restores an authorized remote.

RTP-01 CONTRACTS, POLICIES, AND STORAGE
- Version immutable strategies, policies, runs, scanner runs, candidates,
  completed bars, quotes, setups, AI reviews, intents, approvals, decisions,
  leases, risk reservations, broker events, bracket groups, and reconciliation.
- Keep ORB_V1 as the only active strategy. The registry supports future
  immutable strategies but they must be separately evidenced and receipted.
- Migrate existing V1 databases non-destructively and preserve archived data.
- Move test fakes under backend/tests/fakes and enforce a runtime import
  firewall.

RTP-02 REAL IBKR ACTOR
- Use the official pinned TWS Python API client through one IBKRTwsActor.
- Actor owns socket lifecycle, request IDs, scanner, historical bars, quotes,
  callbacks, account snapshots, subscriptions, and reconnects.
- Validate API handshake, expected managed PAPER account, client-ID availability,
  scanner support, real-time entitlement, and data-line capacity before a run.
- Request real RTH TRADES historical bars for ATR and 20-day dollar-volume
  warmup. A capacity or data-quality failure blocks the run.
- Reconcile positions, open orders, executions, and known bracket legs before
  entries. Unknown or mismatched state blocks new entries.

RTP-03 SCHEDULER, AI, AND RISK
- Acquire a durable scheduler lease and persist one terminal decision per
  candidate. Restart must reuse stable decision identity and never duplicate.
- Run ScannerRun -> Candidate -> CompletedBar/Quote -> RuleSetup -> AIReview
  -> RiskApproval -> Decision.
- Use the Anthropic Claude API with schema-validated output. Enforce a
  maximum of 20 calls and 5 USD per session.
  Invalid, unavailable, slow, or over-budget AI becomes AI_BLOCKED; there is
  no rules-only fallback.
- Create single-use 15-second risk approvals and atomically reserve exposure.

RTP-04 PAPER AUTHORITY AND EXECUTION
- Sign and verify AuthorityReceipt payloads with Ed25519. Signing keys live in
  the local macOS Keychain; runtime uses a pinned public key and never prints
  key material.
- Enter paper_active only after receipt, preflight, scheduler, risk, and
  reconciliation all pass. A reconnect repeats every check before entries can
  resume.
- IBKRTwsPaperExecutor persists the group, legs, reservation, idempotency key,
  owner, and correlations before broker submission.
- Bracket order sequence: parent Transmit=False, target Transmit=False, stop
  Transmit=True last; target and stop share an OCA group. Entry is DAY; stop
  and target are GTC so protection survives an engine crash.
- Session-end safety: cancel unfilled entries at the entry cutoff; flatten
  open positions at close minus 15 minutes (half-day aware) by repricing the
  existing target order to a marketable limit. Never submit a second sell.
- Expected lifecycle events (entry fill, partial fill, target/stop fill, OCA
  sibling cancel) are normal transitions. Unknown, rejected, duplicate,
  timed-out, or mismatched state is MANUAL_INTERVENTION. It fences entries and
  never auto-retries or repairs.

RTP-05 SHADOW, DRILL, AND PAPER CANARY
- Shadow uses only real IBKR data. Modelled analytics are explicitly labelled:
  buy at ask plus 5 bps, sell at bid minus 5 bps, plus a frozen commission
  policy. Modelled fills are never broker-fill evidence.
- PAPER eligibility (owner decision 2026-10-12, light gate): one complete
  real-data shadow session reviewed by the owner, the execution-safety review,
  and the supervised drill below. The strategy-quality thresholds (ten
  sessions, 100 decisions, PF >= 1.20, max drawdown <= 3 percent, AI
  expectancy >= 95 percent of rule-only) are measured during PAPER and gate
  any future real-money work, not PAPER.
- A real PAPER drill must prove order IDs, permIds, orderRefs, OCA linkage,
  restart reconciliation, sibling cancellation, and an observed protected
  partial fill.
- Only then issue the exact-SHA ORB_V1 PAPER receipt. New strategies repeat
  the evidence and drill gates.

OPERATOR CONTROLS
- States: blocked, shadow_ready, paper_ready, paper_active.
- Local command: autotraderctl paper stop. It persists the kill state and
  immediately prevents new entries.
- Dashboard is read-only except emergency stop. It cannot start automation,
  promote authority, edit policies, or submit manual orders.

TESTS AND EVIDENCE
- Verify import firewall, TWS account/port/client checks, entitlement quality,
  scanner and pacing failures, reconnects, ORB timing and price boundaries,
  AI schema and budget limits, risk concurrency, scheduler leases, broker
  sequencing, reconciliation, and protected-partial-fill behavior.
- Every authority increase requires docs/evidence/<SOURCE_SHA>/ evidence.
  Green tests alone are incomplete.
