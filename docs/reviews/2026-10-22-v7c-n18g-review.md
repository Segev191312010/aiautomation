## 1. rule_day_risk_budget (session_check rule 7)

**It parses the real lines.**
- The bridge logs, for the bot (`process_decision`) and for an owner buy (`ManualDesk.buy`), the line `v7c_budget_ok SYM (LABEL): realized X + open Y + new Z = T (P%) <= 3% of N (source=bot|manual)`.
  - The amounts come from `money()`, quantized to the cent, and are never negative.
  - P is quantized to 0.01 and the cap is `Decimal("3")`.
- `_BUDGET_OK` (`session_check.py:211-216`) matches exactly that.
- `test_paper_bridge.py::test_v7c_budget_lines_parse_in_session_check` feeds the bridge's actual stdout lines through the parser and the pairing keys. That is the production-shape meta-test.
- A negative amount or an extra field reads as malformed, which FAILs (fail closed).

**FAIL / WARN.**
- These FAIL, each read in `parse_budget_ok` (`:1412-1440`) and `_rule_day_risk_budget` (`:1443`):
  - a malformed line;
  - parts that do not sum to the total;
  - a source/label mismatch;
  - a logged cap other than 3 %;
  - `max(total, parts) > 3 % x N + 0.01`;
  - a connect line with another cap or factor;
  - a SENT of a v7c process without a line;
  - a `v7c_budget_invariant_broken` page.
- An entry of a process that logged no budget rule is `day_risk_budget_unverified`, a WARN and never a PASS.
- Over-budget, widened-stop and wrong-thread pages are WARNs. I agree: they can happen with no entry breaking the rule.
- Mutants confirm the tests hold this: the builder's M06, M11 and M13 and my Q10 (connect-line cap) are killed.

**Pairing.**
- A bot SENT is keyed by (symbol, identity); an owner buy's `MANUAL SENT BUY … #short (order N)` by its short id. Each is paired first-in-first-out with a budget line logged before it.
- A restart's status follow-up `SENT … (orders [...])` is correctly not taken as a new buy.

**Can a mis-pairing hide an over-3 % entry? No.** Every budget line is judged on its own numbers: an over-3 % line FAILs whether or not it pairs.

**What a mis-pairing *can* hide is an entry sent with no check of its own:**

### N18g-LOW-1: the pending budget lines are not per process (`session_check.py:1462-1477`)

**Facts:** `pending` is keyed by (symbol, identity) or short id for the whole section, not per run. A budget line logged by a process that stopped or disconnected before its SENT stays pending. A later process's SENT for the same key is then "verified" by it, even if that later process logged no budget line at all.

**Probe Q1** (`n18g/probe/backend/tests/test_n18g_probes.py`):
1. Take the fixture day.
2. Insert a new v7c process (sha line, connect line, budget-rule line) between AAA's `v7c_budget_ok` and its `SENT`.
3. Result: rule 7 still says **PASS** ("4 accepted entries … within 3%"). It should FAIL "sent without its v7c_budget_ok line".

**Failure scenario:** the exact regression rule 7 exists to catch. A future bridge whose restart path skips `budget_check` would pass rule 7 whenever the decision's earlier attempt had logged a line before a disconnect.

**Fix:** key `pending` by `(line.run, key)`. The bridge only re-sends a decision from a new process after logging a new line, so a line never legitimately vouches across processes. Add Q1 as a test.

**Rounding (INFO).** Each part is rounded to the cent. A true total within 1.5 cents of the cap could read up to 0.015 over and FAIL (`total > limit + 0.01`). That is fail-closed and practically unreachable.

## 3. The v7c report (`trading/research/v7c_report.py`)

**Blocked trades.**
- Bot rows come from skipped brackets whose `skip_reason` starts with a block token. Manual rows come from `rejected` ledger rows whose `reject_code` is a token, read `?mode=ro`. That matches the bridge's `text.split(":", 1)[0]` code.
- The join is the promoter's own `read_shadow_outcomes`.
- `no_outcome` is counted and never 0 R; `tally` sums only rows with a value.
- `not_filled` counts as 0 R and is shown apart (builder's open question 1). **I agree with 0 R:** a setup that would not have filled would have done nothing, so 0 R is the honest counterfactual. The separate count keeps it visible.
- Manual rows are always `no_outcome` (no setup).
- **I-1 (test gap):** my Q07 survives. A tracker row "filled with no R", recorded as `filled` with R None instead of `no_outcome`, changes only the JSON `outcome` field, not the counts or text. Add one assertion.

**Stop slippage.**
- `stop_ratio` = −realized / (filled x (avg_entry − intent stop)), using the bridge's own realized formula.
- It is excluded, and the exclusion counted, when the risk is ≤ 0, a field is unknown, nothing filled, or exit ≠ entry shares. My Q08 is killed.
- p90 uses nearest rank (an observed value; on n ≤ 10 it is at or near the max), and the flag fires when p90 **>** the factor. **Nearest rank is the right, conservative choice for small samples.**
- Measuring against the intent stop is the plan's definition. Gaps 2 and 3 bias it (an owner-raised stop reads as less slippage), and the report says so.

**P&L by source.** Bot figures come from the live R ledger (gross, commissions, net). Manual is `None`, "unknown (not recorded)". That is honest, since the bridge does not persist owner realized P&L.

**Outputs.**
- `evidence.json["v7c"]` is always present: a failed or missing report writes `readable: false`.
- Section 4 of the weekly text and `evidence.txt` never shows an empty section ("no v7c data …").
- M08, M19 and the builder's tests cover this.

**Pinned copies.** The tokens, `DAY_RISK_BUDGET_PCT` and `STOP_SLIPPAGE_FACTOR` are copies. `test_the_report_vocabulary_is_the_pinned_bridges` loads the bridge, pins each copy, and checks that `BLOCK_TOKENS` is exactly the set of refusal tokens `budget_check` can return. That is the session_check idiom. A reviewed change to the factor must change the copy and its pin together, and the test enforces that.
