### N15d LOW-1 (residual of N15c LOW-1, not a regression): a `telegram` child that takes over late trusts an outage that was current at 16:15, so a new outage that opened while nobody watched is not paged in the evening

- **Where:**
  - `notify_service.py:501-510`: `gateway_outage_current` judges the stamp against
    `SESSION_END` only, never against the time of the take-over.
  - `overnight_watch.py:411-431`: `_take_over` runs once a day at the first step at or after
    16:15, whenever that is, and carries the outage paged.
  - `gateway_watch.py:375`: a carried paged outage is never paged again.
  - The new test pins this behaviour for a 16:40 start
    (`test_overnight_watch.py:426`), and the docs say so (OPERATOR_PROMPT.md:712, "The same rule
    holds for a `telegram` child that was down at 16:15 and takes over later").
- **Evidence:** `probes/test_probe_n15d.py::test_probe_late_takeover_after_an_unwatched_reopen_and_reclose`.
  - Setup: the notifier paged an outage since 16:05 and stamped it at 16:14:59, so it was current.
    The port reopened at 16:30 and closed again at 16:45, until 23:00.
  - Child up at 16:10, which is correct: `✅ … open again 16:30 ET (closed since 16:05 ET)`, then
    `⛔ IB Gateway closed since 16:45 ET …`, then `✅ … open again 23:00 ET (closed since 16:45 ET)`.
  - Child down at 16:15 and started at 17:00: only `✅ IB Gateway port open again 23:00 ET (closed
    since 16:05 ET)`. The 16:45 outage is never paged, and the recovery line misdates it.
- **Why only LOW:** it needs the always-on `telegram` child to be down across 16:15, plus a
  reopen and re-close of the port before it returns and before 18:30. After 18:30 a new outage
  would only be a digest entry anyway. The 05:55 lead still pages a port that is still closed.
  There is no order impact (the bridge fails closed on a closed port). The watch's own restart
  memory (`port_outage_*`) has the same property, which N15c recorded as "same class". f656cba
  behaved the same or worse: it always carried.
- **Fix (owner's choice; both small):**
  - when the take-over is later than `SESSION_END + GATEWAY_OUTAGE_FRESH_FOR`, carry the outage's
    start with `paged=False`, or ignore it. A port still closed is then paged again, possibly a
    duplicate of the notifier's page, which is the loud direction;
  - or keep today's choice and state it in 4.9: "a child that takes over late cannot know what
    happened between 16:15 and its start".
