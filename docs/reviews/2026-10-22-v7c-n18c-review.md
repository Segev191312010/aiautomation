### The checks the coordinator asked for

**LOW-3 baseline: an upper bound that "can only wait longer"?** True for everything the desk knows; early only through an untracked concurrent buy.
- The baseline is at least the true pre-buy position for every trade the desk tracks: unshown owner sells are not subtracted, and other owner buys' unshown fills are added.
- A concurrent owner TWS sale lowers the report, so the rule waits longer (fail closed).
- After a restart, a buy placed before it has no baseline (`buy_baselines` lives in memory). Only the stamp rule applies: probe P18 shows unshown stays 4 and nothing is cancelled.
- Early acceptance needs a buy the desk does not track (an owner TWS-direct buy on the same contract) whose update arrives first. Probe P17: TWS buy of 100 reported, then the `/buy`'s 10-share execution. The fill is accepted as shown at once, so the budget counts 100 held (1,000.00) and misses the `/buy`'s 50.10 until the `/buy`'s own position update arrives.
- The stop fit is not affected in that case: the fit target stays the buy's own count, since held is at least the baseline plus the fills.
- So early acceptance means a transient under-count in the same coincidence the stamp rule already accepts. Acceptable.

## New findings

### N18c-LOW-1: the 60 s book page misses the lost-answer wedge; during it every tick waits the full timeout twice (`paper_bridge.py:2545-2580`, `:2627-2650`, `:2666-2690`)

Facts.
- In the wedge one request is never answered, so `open_orders_answered` stays one behind `open_orders_requested`.
- `watch_budget` sends a new refresh every tick, because `order_book_at` never advances, plus the unprotected recheck. TWS answers each one, and each end moves the counter.
- So `answered + 1` is always the request just sent. Its age is never 60 s, and the page never fires.
- Each of those requests also waits its full timeout: 3 s each, 2 per tick while a position is unprotected.

Probe P9 (test timeouts 0.5 s): 40 ticks, 86 requests, 85 answers, no `v7c_order_book_unavailable` page, last good book None. The sell is refused `open_orders_unavailable`.

Failure scenario. TWS drops one `reqAllOpenOrders` answer and stays connected. For the rest of the session:
- the owner's Telegram sells and `/closeall` are refused, with no page explaining why;
- positions protected only by TWS stops block every entry;
- every tick is about 6 s longer: management, the 15:45 flatten timing and decision intake (15 s approval window) all slow down.

Minimal fix. Do not send a new open-orders request while one is owed: `refresh_order_book` returns None at once while `answered < requested`. A lost answer then ages and pages at 60 s, the per-tick waits disappear, and a merely slow TWS is unchanged. Alternative: page on the age of the last completed book rather than of the oldest unanswered request number. Add P9 as a test.

### N18c-LOW-2: the `settling` arm of `sizing_stops` is untested (mutant J05 survives) (`paper_bridge.py:3229-3236`)

Dropping `self.settling` from `sizing_stops` survives `test_paper_bridge` + `test_promotion_proposals` + `test_v7b_quantities` + `test_supervisor`. The arm only suppresses a false oversized page while a finished buy's fills are settling, so a regression would bring back a false CRITICAL, not hide a real one. Fix: one test, a cancelled partial buy whose fills are not yet shown, with no oversized page.

## The builder's open point 1: the sell-side mirror of LOW-3

My judgement: **do not block. Ride in the deploy head only as the exact mirror of LOW-3 with two tests; otherwise wait for the next layer.**

It is worse than "under-counted": the remaining shares can drop out of O2 entirely.
- Probe P19: 100 AAPL held with no stop, which is unprotected and blocks.
- A `/sell 50` is sent; TWS reports the position (50) before the execution.
- `unsettled_qty` stays 50, so section 3's `other = 50 - 50 = 0`.
- AAPL disappears from the open risk, and its 50 unprotected shares no longer block entries, until the next AAPL position update.
- `check_unsettled` pages CRITICAL at 16 s ("no position update 16s after a fill of 50 sh ... those shares stay reserved").

Why not a blocker:
- The rule behind it (`unsettled_fills`) has run since v7a for bot exits and owner sells alike. The 7 real exit fills in the 10-13..10-20 logs produced 0 lag alerts, so the reversed ordering has not been seen.
- When it happens, a CRITICAL pages within 15 s.
- It predates fix 2. Its budget effect dates from e63a599, which first used `unsettled_sold` in section 3.

Fix: the mirror of LOW-3. A sell's fills count as settled once the reported position is at most its baseline minus the fills. As an interim, extend the `check_unsettled` page to say the budget may not count that contract's remaining shares.
