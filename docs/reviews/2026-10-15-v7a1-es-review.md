## Findings (defects first)

F-1 LOW — the opener's retry wraps `sqlite3.connect()` only; a transient `unable to open database file` raised at
the first statement is not retried. `paper_bridge.py:801-808`. Probe P4: WAL DB, `-shm` absent, read-only
directory → `connect()` returns in 0.000 s, `execute(KILL_FENCE_SQL)` raises `OperationalError: unable to open
database file`; every caller then fails closed exactly as before v7a.1 (SKIP / `PREFLIGHT_REFUSED`), so this is an
availability gap, not a safety one, and the live directory is writable (the artefact did not reproduce there).
Required fix: none for this deploy. At the v7b re-pin, if the retry is to mean what the docstring says, run one
`connection.execute("SELECT 1 FROM sqlite_schema LIMIT 1")` inside the retry loop before returning the connection
(same three attempts), or narrow the docstring to "a connect-time open failure".

F-2 LOW — worst-case latency added between the last expiry check and `placeOrder`. `process_decision`
(`:1588-1609`): `bid()` (≤ 4 s) → `_expiry_problem` → kill read → identity read → `submit`. Probe P5 with
`connect` failing twice per read: 0.516 s + 0.521 s = 1.04 s after the last expiry check (previously ~0 ms); a
decision can leave up to ~1 s past its 15 s approval only if the DB is being replaced at that instant. The
decision intake adds ≤ 0.5 s per tick while a CANTOPEN condition persists (management runs first in `tick()`
`:2009-2022`, so cancels/resizes/flatten are not delayed; the manual desk's `live_facts` read treats the raise as
`kill=True`). Judged acceptable against the 15 s TTL and `MAX_DECISION_AGE_S = 60`. Optional hardening at the
v7b re-pin: re-run `_expiry_problem` after the two fence reads (one line, keeps the fence "as fresh as possible"
invariant and makes the TTL exact).

F-3 INFO — `Caps` accepts a last-entry offset as small as 1 s (P1). Harmless: `entry_deadline` (`:1251-1253`)
takes `min(times.last_entry, closes_at − offset)`, so no row can place an entry after 15:30 (the v7a review's
suggested ≥ 30 min floor would be cosmetic). No change requested.

F-4 INFO (outside the diff) — `import copy` (`paper_bridge.py:129`) has no use in the file. Pre-existing; leave it
for the v7b re-pin rather than touch bridge bytes now.

Informational: the new `test_v7a1_the_shared_read_only_opener…` monkeypatches `pb.sqlite3.connect`, i.e. the
process-wide `sqlite3.connect`, and `pb.time.sleep`; `monkeypatch` restores both, and the targeted and full runs
show no bleed. The commit's docs claim about the artefact is accurate; the LEARNED 10-14 advice to read the live
DB with `immutable=1` has its own hazard (P4: `immutable=1` ignores an un-checkpointed `-wal`, so it showed
