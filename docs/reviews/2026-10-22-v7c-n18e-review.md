## The coordinator's questions

### Does `wait_owed` hold the main loop too long? (builder's open point 2)

**Acceptable.**
- An owner sell can hold the loop at most `sync_timeout` (10 s): one deadline covers both waits (`:2599`). That is the same bound as v7b's 10 s sell snapshot.
- Under a lost-answer wedge, each claimed owner sell costs 10 s once before it is refused. The watch never waits.
- Bot stops and targets are GTC orders at IBKR and do not need the loop.
- The 15:45 flatten is shielded: `FLATTEN_GUARD` (20 s, `:2996`/`:3548`) stops new owner rows being claimed before it, which covers a 10 s snapshot plus a 4 s quote.
- Paths not covered by that guard:
  - a release's second pass, and
  - `silent_verdict`.

  Each can still take one 10 s snapshot inside the guard window. A `/closeall` claimed just before the window also re-reads quotes per holding. That pattern existed before fix 4, and fix 4 does not lengthen any bound.
- A 10 s hold can push a fresh bot decision past its 15 s approval. The entry is then skipped, which fails closed.
- **Test gap:** the shared deadline itself is untested. My mutant P04, which gives the second wait a fresh 10 s, survives (LOW-2).

### Can the fit guard fail to unblock?

It waits until a position update for the contract arrives after the sale's execution. Until then:
- the stop is kept as it is;
- its 15 s CRITICAL tells the owner;
- the stop counts as "sized by the desk", so the oversized page stays silent while the fit waits.

In P20 the next update lifts the guard and the fit completes. That is the intended fail-closed behaviour.

### Can the oversized grace hide a real short for longer than 15 s?

**Yes, while the position keeps changing (LOW-1 below).**

The grace timer is keyed by the quantities: `(symbol, stops, covered)` (`:2686`). Every change of the held or covered count starts a new 15 s.

Probe P23: VEEV's 320-share leg, with 100 held and the owner selling 10 every 10 s in TWS. No page came during the 40 s of changes. The first CRITICAL came at +56 s, 15 s after the last change.

## New findings (LOW; a later layer, as agreed)

### N18e-LOW-1: the oversized grace restarts on every quantity change (`paper_bridge.py:2676-2700`)

**Facts:**
- `oversized_since` and the alert keys are keyed by `(symbol, stops, covered)`.
- A position that changes faster than every 15 s never completes a grace.
- A persistent oversell also re-pages, with new numbers, after each change once it has settled.

**Failure scenario:** an oversized stop (a 10-15 leg, or a `/buy` stop after TWS sales) on a contract the owner is actively trading in TWS. The short-risk page arrives only after trading pauses for 15 s.

**Minimal fix:** time the episode per contract, keyed `(V7C_STOP_OVERSIZED, symbol)`, and keep the quantities only in the text.

### N18e-LOW-2: the desk's shared 10 s deadline is untested (mutant P04 survives) (`paper_bridge.py:2588-2600`)

Giving the desk's own request a fresh full budget after the owed wait passes every bridge test. A regression would let one owner sell hold the loop for 20 s, which equals `FLATTEN_GUARD`.

**Fix:** one test in which the owed answer takes about 9 s of a 10 s budget; assert the total wait stays at or under 10 s and the sell is refused.
