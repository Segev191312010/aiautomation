## Findings

### MEDIUM-1: v7c can leave an owner position with no stop (`paper_bridge.py:3413-3495`, `:3637-3717`, `:3005-3054`)

Path (a): an exit command strips the stop, then cannot sell. Facts.
- `ManualDesk.process` (`:3422-3423`) takes `freeing = self.own_entry_stops(None)` for every `/closeall` before any
  per-holding check; `revalidate_claimed` returns `close_all` without looking at quotes, so `release` (`:3483-3492`)
  cancels every attached stop whose buy is no longer working.
- Only after IBKR confirms the cancels does `close_all` run its per-holding checks (`close_all_problem`, then
  `market_quote_problem` at `:3682`: a real-time book no wider than 1 %, US stock, whole shares, not stopping). A
  holding that fails any of them is "not sold" - and its stop is already gone.
- `/close` and `/sell all` re-validate (quote included) before the release, so they fail only in the window between
  the release and the second pass (`:3443-3455`, then `follow_releases` `:3223-3247`): a quote that widens, the
  90 s message expiry, `can_send` failing (stop request or disconnect).
- `self.releases` is in memory only. A SIGTERM, a TWS disconnect (exit 75) or the 15:55 `finish()` (which runs
  `follow_all` but not `follow_releases`) inside the up-to-10 s release leaves the stop cancelled, nothing sold, and
  the row `sending`, marked `unknown` and never resent at the next start (`ManualDesk.startup` `:3065`).
- v7b behaved differently: with no attached stops, `/closeall` left a stop-reserved holding alone ("reserved by
  working sells/stops - cancel them first"), still protected.

Reproduced (probe P1, `probe/backend/tests/test_n18_probes.py`): a filled `/buy 10 AAPL stop 185`, then
`/closeall` with the book 1.06 % wide: `broker.cancelled == [stop_id]`, `broker.placed == []`, row
`rejected nothing_closed` "AAPL: spread 1.06% over 1% - not sold", and `account_open_risk().unprotected` names AAPL.
P1b: `/close AAPL` whose quote widens during the release: stop cancelled, row `rejected spread_too_wide`.

Failure scenario (a). The owner's panic button during the incident class of 2026-10-13 (a phone or web login takes the
API market data: only delayed quotes): `/closeall` cancels the GTC stop of every owner position bought with `/buy`,
sells none of them ("only a delayed quote"), and leaves all of them with no stop into the close and overnight. The
only signal is the `v7c_unprotected_position` page 15 s later, whose advice (`/close SYM`) fails for the same reason.

Path (b): the stop fit cancels the stop of a filled buy on a lagging position. Facts.
- When a buy's track ends (`filled` included, `:3884-3887`) `fit_later` schedules its attached stop for fitting
  `STOP_FIT_GRACE` (= 15 s) later (`:2793`, `:2994`). `tend_stop_fits` then takes
  `held = owner_shares(con_id)` (account position - unsettled sells - bot shares, `:3033`) and
  `target = min(remaining, held, bought)` (`:3034`); a target of 0 cancels the stop (`:3039`).
- A position update that has not arrived 15 s after the fill reads as 0 held: the stop of shares just bought is
  cancelled. The bridge already alerts such lags for sells (`check_unsettled`, `POSITION_LAG_ALERT` 15 s), so the
  lag is a known failure mode, not a hypothesis.
- Reproduced (probe P8): `/buy 10 AAPL stop 185` fills, no position update for 18 s: `broker.cancelled == [stop]`,
  log `STOP CANCELLED AAPL order 101: its buy is over and holds nothing it could sell (0 sh held)`.

Failure scenario (b). A TWS that reports the fill but lags the position (as around the 10-15 1100 outage): the
owner's fresh position loses its only stop with no owner action; when the position update arrives, the budget
pages `v7c_unprotected_position` and blocks every entry.

Minimal fix.
- `/closeall`: decide per holding first (position, `close_all_problem`, fresh quote), then release only the stops of
  holdings it will sell; or release per holding right before its own sell.
- Stop fit: use the position only once a position update stamped after the buy's last fill has arrived (the
  `positions_seen`/`position_seq` rule of `unsettled_fills`), else re-check later; never cancel on an unconfirmed
  zero. Test: P8 as a regression test.
- `/close`, `/sell all`: re-check the quote and `can_send` immediately before `cancelOrder`, and on any refusal after
  a release, a CRITICAL that names the symbol ("your stop on SYM was cancelled and SYM was not sold - it has no stop;
  act in TWS"), not the generic 15 s page.
- Persist the release (the row's audit already has `releasing_stops`): at startup, a `sending` row with
  `releasing_stops` is reported CRITICAL by name instead of a plain `unknown`.
- Tests: a closeall with one wide-quote holding keeps that holding's stop; a release interrupted by a stop request.

### MEDIUM-2: a timed-out book refresh makes the next one partial; owner sells can then oversell (`paper_bridge.py:1186`, `:2454-2466`, `:2511-2530`, `:3271-3274`)

Facts.
- `refresh_order_book` clears `account_orders`, clears `open_orders_done`, calls `reqAllOpenOrders()` and waits for
  the event. `openOrderEnd` (`:1186`) only sets that one event: it carries no request id.
- After a timeout the request stays outstanding at TWS. If its answer straddles the next request's clear (part of it
  before, the rest and its `openOrderEnd` after), the next wait returns on the old request's end, and the snapshot is
  only the old answer's tail.
- v7c adds a 3 s-timeout refresh every 30 s (`ORDER_BOOK_TIMEOUT_S`, `ORDER_BOOK_REFRESH`) and, while any position
  is unprotected, every ~5 s (`UNPROTECTED_RECHECK`). The owner desk's sell snapshot (`open_orders_snapshot`, 10 s)
  is the same function and the same event, and runs in the same tick right after the watch.
- Existed in principle in v7b (two 10 s-timeout users, sells and `silent_verdict`), now routine whenever TWS answers
  in more than 3 s.

Reproduced (probe P3): TWS answers from its own thread (the owner's TWS stop on 100 AAPL after 0.3 s, then another
order and `openOrderEnd` 0.4 s later). The watch refresh times out at 0.5 s; the desk then claims `/sell 50 AAPL`
(100 held, the stop reserving all 100). Result: `bridge.order_book` = `[501 TSLA]` (the AAPL stop missing), and
`SELL 50 AAPL MKT` is sent: 150 shares of sells on 100 shares.

Failure scenario. A slow TWS (Gateway degradation as on 2026-10-16, an 1100 period): the owner sends `/sell` or
`/closeall`; the reservation misses a working stop; the sell fills, then the stop fires: the account is short. The
same partial snapshot also feeds `close_all_problem`.

Minimal fix. Count requests and ends under `app.lock` (`requested += 1` per `reqAllOpenOrders`/`reqOpenOrders`,
`ends += 1` in `openOrderEnd`) and treat a snapshot as complete only when `ends >= requested` for this request; while
an earlier request is unanswered, return None (a sell is then refused `open_orders_unavailable`, the budget treats
the book as stale): fail closed. Test: the P3 probe as a regression test.

### MEDIUM-3: the fail-closed branches of O2 and of the reservation are untested (10 fail-open mutants survive)

Facts (mutation table below; each mutant re-pinned so that only a behavioural test can kill it). Coverage (O2):
N01 a cancelled own stop still covers; N02 an STP LMT covers; N03 another account's stop covers; N04 own orders
counted again from the all-clients book; N05 a short is not unprotected; N06 an unknown avgCost counts zero risk
instead of unprotected; N08 a stop on 1 share covers 2,941; N09 a partly filled stop covers its whole total.
Realized and reservation: N13 the computed realized loss also counts owner BUY executions (an add above the cost
basis books a phantom gain that offsets real losses); N21 an owner buy placed but not yet reported by IBKR is not
reserved (the fake broker echoes every status synchronously, so the `status is None` arm never runs). All survive
`test_paper_bridge.py` + `test_promotion_proposals.py` + `test_v7b_quantities.py` + `test_supervisor.py` (417 tests).
The builder's M12 ("unprotected position ignored") dies only because it removes the whole refusal.

Failure scenario. Any later edit of `covering_stops`, `stop_cover`, `account_open_risk`, `computed_realized` or
`pending_entries` (P4's port of these semantics to the C2 engine is planned, O9) can turn a fail-closed branch into
fail-open with every test green: the budget then counts an uncovered or short position as covered, or a loss as
offset, and entries continue on an account whose real risk is not bounded by 3 %.

Minimal fix. One test per branch asserting the refusal or the counted risk (no production change): own stop
`Cancelled`; `STP LMT` and `TRAIL` in the book; another account's stop; a client-18 order also present in the book
(`own_in_all_open_orders=True`) with held = 2x its quantity; a short; avgCost 0/unset; stops summing to held - 1; a
stop with partial fills; a `manual:` BOT execution next to a SLD one; a buy whose status echo has not arrived.

### LOW-1: an owner buy that has filled leaves the budget until reqPositions shows it (`paper_bridge.py:2970-2988`, `:3884-3887`, `:2307-2333`)

`pending_entries` counts a buy only while its track lives; `follow` pops the track as soon as executions reach the
quantity (`filled`), and section 3 counts the shares only once `reqPositions` shows them. Probe P2: open risk
50.10 (working) -> **0** (filled, position not yet shown) -> 50.10 (position shown). A bot decision (or a second
owner buy) checked in that gap is under-counted by up to one owner buy's risk (at most 1 % NLV). The bridge already
treats this lag as real for sells (`unsettled_fills`, 15 s `POSITION_LAG_ALERT`). Fix: keep a filled buy's
reservation until a position update stamped after its last fill (the `positions_seen` rule `unsettled_fills` uses),
or count BOT executions of `manual:` refs not yet in the position as held at their price to the attached stop.

### LOW-2: a quiet reqPnL drops a known loss after 120 s (`paper_bridge.py:2352-2380`, `:1277-1285`)

`account_realized_loss` uses IBKR's value only while it is at most 120 s old, else the computed sum (bot brackets +
`manual:` sell executions). If TWS sends `pnl()` only when a value changes (spike V-2 left the cadence unverified), a
flat account goes quiet and a TWS-direct or carried-position loss (the 10-15 IP/VEEV legs are not today's brackets
and not `manual:`) vanishes from both the 3 % term and the -2 % block. Probe P4: reqPnL -14,000 (2.1 %) blocks; the
same value 121 s old does not, and a MOMO decision is sent. Fix: keep the last reqPnL realized of this connection as
a lower bound for the loss regardless of age (`max(0, -min(last_reported, computed))`), log staleness separately;
check the `v7c_pnl_source` flips in the first session's bridge.log.

### LOW-3: unfilled bot shares are subtracted from the account position (`paper_bridge.py:2290`, `:2316`)

`claimed` adds `held + pending`, but the position holds only `held`. If the owner buys the same contract in TWS while
a bot entry is still working, part of the owner's shares is not counted. Probe P5: 40 of 100 bot shares filled,
owner +100 in TWS, owner stop at 40: only "40 held" counted (risk 400, true 1,000). Fix: `claimed += held`.

### LOW-4: a second unprotected episode is never paged (`paper_bridge.py:2498-2509`, `alert_once` `:1469`)

`page_unprotected` forgets the episode in `unprotected_since` but `alert_once` keeps the key in `self.alerted`, so the
same position becoming unprotected again with the same text is silent all day (probe P6: 1 page, then 0), while every
entry is refused and the SKIP lines are not forwarded to the phone. Fix: discard the alert key when the episode ends.

### LOW-5: the unprotected advice cannot always be followed (`UNPROTECTED_ADVICE` `:246`)

"close it with /close SYM" is refused (`bot_holds_symbol`) when a past session's bot leg still works on the symbol
(IP/VEEV if any 10-15 leg survives) and (`bot_needs_attention`) for a `needs_attention` bot bracket. A
`needs_attention` bracket with fills also keeps O3's block all session even after the owner closes the shares in TWS
(its held comes from its own legs' fills, never from the account position). Fix: add "or act in TWS"; cap section 1's
held by the account position after the position grace.

### LOW-6: the P3-B0 pin covers the three direct lazy imports, not what they import (`supervisor.py:251-255`)

`manual_orders.py` imports `db.core` (`get_db`, `transaction` = `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, the claim's atomicity) and
`trading.scheduler.sessions`; `db.core` imports `trading.storage.migrations` and every schema module; `config.py`
imports `trading.operations.paths` (the DB location); importing `trading.scheduler` runs its `__init__` (lease,
decision_keys). Exactly the plan's three files, so not a deviation; C1's reason applies to these too. Fix (later
layer): pin the closure computed by a test (AST walk from the three entry points over `BACKEND_ROOTS`), or at least
`db/core.py` and `trading/operations/paths.py`.

### LOW-7: on a `/buy` position Telegram can neither sell part nor tighten the stop (`paper_bridge.py:3404-3411`)

The attached stop reserves its shares, so `/sell N` and `/stop SYM PRICE` are refused `insufficient_shares`; only
`/close`, `/sell all`, `/closeall` release it (the builder's own test calls it an open question). Safe (never two

only through TWS. `/close` while the owner's own buy still works is refused (`manual_buy_still_working`) - two steps

### LOW-8: an oversized stop counts as covering (`paper_bridge.py:2320`)

Coverage needs stops >= held; it never flags stops > held. VEEV's 10-15 leg (320 sh) "covers" 100 held (probe P7b):
if it fires the account goes short 220. Fix: a WARN (not a block) when working sell stops exceed the shares held.

## The ten checks

1. **Budget arithmetic and sources.** `position_risk` = held x max(0, avg - stop) x f + pending x max(0, limit -
   stop) x f; the new risk also carries f (`added = new_risk * STOP_SLIPPAGE_FACTOR`). `current_stop` reads
   `order_objects[stop_id].auxPrice` (STP, > 0) else the intent; the cadence refresh re-reports client 18's own orders
   too, so an owner TWS edit is seen within 30 s whether or not TWS pushes it (V-1), and `note_stops` logs the first
   difference (`v7c stop SYM order N: intent X, at IBKR Y`), which answers V-1 at the first edit. No report yet ->
   intent, `v7c_stop_unconfirmed` (INFO, once per bracket) from the watch (O5). `account_open_risk` sums (1) bot
   brackets (`held` at avg once filled, else limit; `pending` while the entry may fill; a held bracket with a dead
   stop leg is unprotected, O3), (2) owner buys still working (unfilled x (limit or ask - attached stop)), (3) every
   other long at avgCost from `reqPositions` to the lowest working SELL STP (own non-leg orders by status; other
   clients' from the book while <= 90 s old; STP only; this account), all shares covered or unprotected; a short is
   unprotected. Partial fills and unfilled entries: tested. Realized: the larger loss of fresh reqPnL and computed,
   WARN on a gap > max($25, 0.05 % NLV). Gaps: LOW-1, LOW-2, LOW-3; untested branches MEDIUM-3.
2. **Atomicity.** `process_decision`: gate, bid wait (<= 4 s), expiry re-check, kill fence, identity fence, then
