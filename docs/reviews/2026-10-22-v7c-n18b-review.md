## New findings

### N18b-LOW-1: the oversized-stop CRITICAL fires while the owner's buy is still working, and advises cancelling its stop (`paper_bridge.py:2353-2354`, `:2593-2604`)

Facts. `account_open_risk` records a contract as oversized when `covered > other` (`:2353`). `covered` includes every
live own SELL STP, so the attached stop of a `/buy` that is still working counts in full: it is `PreSubmitted`,
its total is the whole buy, and the buy's parentId is not consulted. `warn_oversized` then pages CRITICAL
when stops exceed the account position: "... if they all fire the account is short N sh — resize or cancel the extra
stop in TWS".

Reproduced:
- Probe P15: `/buy 10 AAPL 185 stop 180`, 4 filled, the buy still working. CRITICAL "working stops sell 10 sh but
  cover 4 held: if they all fire the account is short 6 sh — resize or cancel the extra stop in TWS". A new page
  fires at each partial-fill step.
- Probe P15b: an unfilled `/buy 10` next to 100 held under the owner's own TWS stop. CRITICAL "... short 10 sh". A
  child of an unfilled parent cannot fire, so the page is plainly false.

Failure scenario. Normal `/buy` use produces CRITICAL pages that tell the owner to resize or cancel the stop that is
protecting the buy. If he does, the shares the buy fills next have no stop. The budget then pages
`v7c_unprotected_position` and blocks entries, but the position itself is unprotected.

Minimal fix. Leave out of the oversize sum, or at least out of the CRITICAL, the attached stops whose parent buy
still works (its parentId's status is in `WORKING_ORDER_STATUSES`), or add that buy's unfilled shares to `other`.
Add a test for each of the two probes.

### N18b-LOW-2: one open-orders answer TWS never sends wedges every later refresh, silently, until a restart (`paper_bridge.py:1201`, `:2512-2536`)

Facts. With the counting rule, one request TWS never answers keeps `open_orders_answered` one behind
`open_orders_requested`. Every later end then completes the request before it, so no request completes again on
that connection. This is the builder's open point 2.

Probe P9: the first `reqAllOpenOrders` is dropped and every later one is answered. Five refreshes all return None;
requested 6, answered 5. The owner's `/sell 10 AAPL` is refused `open_orders_unavailable`. No CRITICAL is
logged.

Consequences while the wedge lasts:
- Every owner Telegram sell, `/close` and `/closeall` is refused.
- After 90 s the budget's book goes stale, so positions protected only by TWS stops read as unprotected. That blocks
  entries, but it also pages (the page names "no fresh open-orders book").
- Bot brackets are unaffected: their legs and the flatten do not read the book.

My judgement on open point 2: not writing the request off is right; a write-off would bring the MEDIUM-2 race back.
But the session should not stay wedged with no signal.

Minimal fix:
- A CRITICAL once when a request has been owed more than about 60 s. It should name the effect: "Telegram sells and
  /closeall are refused, TWS stops are unknown to the budget; act in TWS".
- Optionally exit 75 for a supervised reconnect when it is owed more than a few minutes and no release or owner sell
  is in flight. A new connection resets both counters, and no stale end can cross it.

Is it likely? It needs TWS to drop a request while the socket stays up. That has not been observed, and the old
design had no such state, so I rate it LOW.

### N18b-LOW-3: a position update that arrives before the fill's execution keeps the fill "unshown" until the next update (`paper_bridge.py:3117-3137`, `:3192`)

Facts. `unshown_fills` treats an execution as shown only when a position update for its contract arrives *after*
it. The rule is the same one `unsettled_fills` uses for sells. If TWS sends the position first, no later update
arrives (positions are pushed only on change), and the fill stays unshown.

Probe P14: position 4, then the 4-share execution, then the rest of the buy cancelled. After 40 s:
- `unshown` = 4;
- the open risk counts the 4 shares twice ("owner buy 4 not in the position yet ... 20.00" and "account 4 held ...
  20.00");
- the stop fit never completes (the 10-share stop stays on 4 held), with a lag CRITICAL and an oversized "short 6"
  CRITICAL.

Pre-fix code would have fitted the stop to 4.

Evidence that this ordering is rare: the copied bridge logs of 10-13..10-20 hold 7 bot exit fills, all under the
same rule (`check_unsettled` covers bot legs). None produced a "no position update" alert, so execution-then-position
is the observed order.

Failure scenario. In that ordering, an oversized attached stop survives and could sell more than is held. The budget
over-counts, which is fail-closed. The owner is paged twice.

Minimal fix. On the lag CRITICAL, re-subscribe positions (`cancelPositions` + `reqPositions`) so TWS sends a fresh
update and the sequence moves on. Alternatively, treat a fill as shown when the position already equals the
pre-buy position plus the fills.

### N18b-LOW-4: two fail-open mutants of the new LOW-1 code survive (test gaps)

- **G06**: `price = max(price, average_price)` -> `price = price` (`:3113`). Unshown fills count at the sized price
  even when the buy filled higher, as a market buy can. It survives `test_paper_bridge` + `test_promotion_proposals`
  + `test_v7b_quantities` + `test_supervisor`.
- **G08**: the status-count term `unshown += max(0, order_filled - executed)` is dropped (`:3136`). A fill IBKR
  reports by status before its execution is then neither reserved nor gating the stop fit; that is MEDIUM-1b's
  failure in that ordering. It survives the same set.

Fix: one test each (a MKT buy filled above the ask; `orderStatus` Filled before `execDetails`).

### N18b-LOW-5 (pre-existing, the builder's open point 3): a `/closeall` row resumed as `unknown` settles `filled` (`paper_bridge.py:4186`, `:4206`, `:4231`)

Facts. A `/closeall` claimed and then lost to a hard crash before its outcome is written is marked `unknown` at the
next start and resumed with no order ids, no bot ids and no notes. `follow` does not return early for `close_all`
(`:4186`), and `close_all_outcome` finds no shortfall, so the row becomes `filled`. The phone can then show
"Filled CLOSE ALL" with nothing sold. Fix 1 removed the release case (now `error` by name); the general case
remains.

My judgement: real, but it needs a hard kill (SIGTERM finishes the step, and exceptions are written
`unknown`/`error` by `process_safely`), and it predates v7c. Fix later: a `close_all` track resumed with no
children and no bot closes ends `cancelled` with "outcome unknown — check TWS".

### Informational

- **I-1 (open point 1, N15).** I agree that N15 is near-equivalent at flow level on e3af81e. A `/stop` row is always
  counted by the ledger while live, so `/close`, `/sell all` and `/closeall` keep that holding reserved and release
  nothing, mutant or not. The direct test of `own_entry_stops`/`is_entry_stop` is the right kill. It stops being
  enough only if a `/stop` order works at IBKR while its row is terminal, which is not a v7c path.
- **I-2 (open point 2).** The other residual: an end nobody asked for, arriving while a request is outstanding, can't
  be told apart. Accepted. The only such end comes right after connect, when the only outstanding request is
  reconcile's `reqOpenOrders`. The reports are the same content, and TWS answers that request before the executions
  and positions the startup waits for, so its real end arrives with nothing outstanding and is dropped.
- **I-3 (open point 4).** `/closeall` is slower: one quote per holding with an attached stop in the decide pass and
  again in the released pass, before and after the cancels. The 4 s is a timeout; a liquid quote returns at once,
  and a holding without data is kept anyway. Acceptable. The next tick's management can wait that long (the desk
  runs last in a tick).
- **I-4 (reqPnL floor).** The floor is the last finite value IBKR reported on this connection. A newer finite report
  replaces it (probe P4: it lifts), and a restart re-reads it. Relative to the computed source it is only more
  conservative, since it can only add loss. It can overstate only if IBKR's own last value overstates and IBKR then
  goes silent, for example a daily P&L reset while the bridge is connected followed by unset reports. The bridge
  runs 09:25-16:00 ET, so that needs a mid-session reset. That is a false block, never a missed loss.
- **I-5 (`needs_attention` cap).** It can under-count only if the account position lags a fill of the bracket's own
  legs by more than 15 s. A `needs_attention` bracket is never managed and was not found working at startup, so new
  fills on its legs are essentially impossible, and a stale position at startup comes from a fresh `reqPositions`.
  I see no realistic under-count. The grace is tested (G07 killed).
- **I-6 (stop-fit wait).** "Never fitted" happens only in LOW-3's ordering or with a lasting mismatch between status
  count and executions. "Oversized" (rather than cancelled) is the chosen side; it is paged twice. Acceptable.
- **I-7 (gates).** My full suite shows 3640 passed, 2 skipped; the builder's shows 3641 passed, 1 skipped. Both total
  3642. My extra skip is `test_size_history`, which needs the git history the archive does not have.
