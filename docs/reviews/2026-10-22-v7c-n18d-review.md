## New findings

### N18d-LOW-1: under a slow TWS, owner sells are refused while the watch's request is owed (`paper_bridge.py:2544-2590`, `:3626`)

**Facts:**
- The watch's book refresh times out at 3 s. If TWS answers in 3-10 s, the request stays owed until the late answer.
- Meanwhile every other refresh returns None at once, including the desk's sell, `/close`, `/closeall` and `silent_verdict` snapshots. Each of those used to wait up to 10 s itself.
- While a position is unprotected, the watch re-reads about every 5 s, so under such a TWS the owed windows can cover most of the time.

**Probe P21 (TWS answers in 1 s, watch timeout 0.5 s):** a `/sell` claimed in the window is `rejected open_orders_unavailable`, and the next refresh after the answer completes.

**Failure scenario:** a degraded TWS during an incident. The owner's Telegram exits are refused again and again. This fails closed, so there is no oversell, but exits are delayed. The owner must resend or act in TWS.

**Minimal fix:** callers with the long timeout (the desk, 10 s) should first wait, within their own timeout, for the owed request to be answered, then send their own request. The watch (3 s) keeps returning None at once. This still keeps at most one request outstanding.

**Related test gap:** mutant L02 survives. L02 makes a refresh return the last book (up to 90 s old) instead of None while a request is owed. No test has a fresh book followed by an owed request. Add one.

### N18d-LOW-2: the buy-side twin of fix 2's LOW-3 (two buys, one contract) also raises a false oversized CRITICAL (`paper_bridge.py:2665`; fix 2's `position_holds_the_fills`)

Coordinator's question 4.

**Probe P22:**
- Two `/buy 10 AAPL` orders, A and B, are working. A's position update (10) arrives before A's and B's executions.
- Both buys are then counted as shown (unshown 0/0), while 20 shares are really held.

**Effects:**
- **No stop shrinks or is cancelled** (`cancelled == []`; both stops stay at 10). The fit target is capped at each buy's own count, and acceptance needs the report to hold at least the baseline plus the fills.
- **The budget under-counts B's risk.** It counts "10 held ... 50.00" until B's own position update. This is transient, as the builder says.
- **Not confined to that.** While the report says 10, the LOW-8 check sees 20 shares of stops on 10 held. It pages CRITICAL: "short 10 sh — resize or cancel the extra stop in TWS". This is the same harmful advice N18b-LOW-1 removed for working buys. Following it would leave real shares without a stop. The alert key keeps that page sent for the session even after the report catches up.

**Likelihood:** it needs two concurrent owner buys on one contract and a position update arriving before its execution. That ordering has not been seen in production.

**Minimal fix:** give `warn_oversized` the same persistence grace as `page_unprotected` (POSITION_GAP_GRACE, 15 s) before its CRITICAL. That absorbs every transient report lag. A per-placement fill mark, as the builder proposes, can wait.

**Not a blocker.**

### N18d-LOW-3: the stop fit can cancel a stop on shares really held when a sale's position update came before its execution (`paper_bridge.py:3316`, `owner_shares` `:3686`, section 3 `:2379`)

This is coordinator question 4, second part, and the sell-side mirror's worst consequence.

**Facts:**
- `tend_stop_fits` reads `held = owner_shares(con_id)`, which is the account position minus unsettled sells minus bot shares.
- Fix 1 gates the fit only on the buy's own unshown fills.
- If a sale on the same contract is stuck "unsettled" because its update came first, its shares are subtracted from a position that already excludes them.

**Probe P20:**
1. 50 AAPL are held under a TWS stop.
2. `/buy 10 AAPL stop 185` fills in the normal order.
3. The owner cancels the TWS stop and sends `/sell 50`.
4. The position (10) is reported before the sale's execution.

**Result, 15 s after the buy ended:**
- `STOP CANCELLED AAPL order 101: its buy is over and holds nothing it could sell (0 sh held)`, although 10 shares are really held. That is a MANUAL line; no page names it.
- Section 3 shows nothing for AAPL, so the unprotected 10 shares do not block entries.
- The only CRITICAL is `check_unsettled`'s "no position update 16s after a fill of 50 sh ... those shares stay reserved".

**Scope:**
- This predates fix 3. The fit's `owner_shares` read dates from e63a599.
- It needs the reversed ordering for a sale (0 of 7 real exit fills showed it) and a Telegram sale on the same contract within 15 s of a `/buy` ending.
- It removes a stop without the owner asking, which is the class of N18 MEDIUM-1(b). That is why I recommend the fix ride in the deploy head despite LOW likelihood.

**Minimal fix (fail closed, independent of any mirror design):** `tend_stop_fits` leaves the stop as it is while `unsettled_qty(con_id) > 0` on that contract, as it already does while the buy's own fills are unshown. Add P20 as a test. The sell-side mirror itself (the section-3 under-count) can wait for the per-placement design the builder describes, as I said in N18c. I agree the exact mirror is not clean (the builder's two-sale example).
