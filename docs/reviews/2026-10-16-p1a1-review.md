### L-1 LOW: F-6 is only fixed for the bridge, so an IBKR-side loss the bridge cannot see is still silent, and a TWS restore line can close the alert while market data is still absent

**Where**

**Evidence.** N10's F-6 recommended counting the screener's `sweep failed: N consecutive` as a failure signal. The commit does not. The author's own 10-14 afternoon test feeds 38 `sweep failed` lines, and the alert comes only from the bridge's 1100 line and its re-assertion every 2 min.

### L-2 LOW: the 1100 re-assertion can page a false "unreachable" between 16:00 and 16:15 ET, or after a bridge restart during a loss

**Where.** notify_service.py:1050-1055: `ibkr_lost_at` is cleared only by a sweep or by a 1101/1102.

**Scenario.**
- The bridge logs 1100 at 15:59:00.
- The bridge and the screener windows end at 16:00. The notifier runs until 16:15.
- IBKR restores at 16:00:30, but no client is left to log 1102 and no sweep comes.
- The re-assertion at 16:01:00 opens the alert: "⛔ IB Gateway unreachable since 15:59 ET — log in". No "reconnected" message follows before 16:15.

The same thing can happen in session if the bridge restarts mid-loss: the new client never receives the 1102. In session the next screener sweep clears it, so at most one false pair results.

**Fix.** Stop re-asserting once the bridge's window has ended (or once no bridge is running). Treat a sweep-less window end as "unknown", not "lost".

### F-6/F-7: are the IBKR code meanings right?

- **The meanings are right.**
  - 1100: connectivity between IBKR and TWS lost.
  - 1101: restored, data lost (requests must be resubmitted; the screener and the bridge use snapshots, so nothing is stale).
  - 1102: restored, data maintained.
  - 2110: connectivity between TWS and the server is broken, to be restored automatically.
- **How the bridge logs them.** It already logs 1100 and 2110 as CRITICAL (`CRITICAL_TWS_CODES`, paper_bridge.py:264) and 1101/1102 as INFO (only 2100-2199 non-critical codes are dropped, :1141).
- **Regex anchoring.** The regexes require `TWS <reqId> <code>`. Order lines (`BUY 1100 @`), own-order errors (`TWS 1101 201 …`), MANUAL warnings (`TWS 1100 Connectivity … (order 7)`), 1300, 2104 and the watchdog line all give `None` (probe).
- **False alerts:**
  - L-2: the window end or a bridge restart mid-loss;
  - 2110 on connect, if TWS were to send it while healthy (could not verify; unlikely).
- **Swallowed alerts:**
  - a 1101/1102 is a success, so it restarts the sustain clock and can close an alert while the farms are down (L-1);
  - it cannot arrive while the port is closed, so it cannot hide a port outage.
- **Double counting.** One attempt is now one signal, which only lowers sensitivity back to its intended level. 1100 and 2110 arriving together count as 2 signals, which only makes the alert open sooner.

## F-1 recommendation: keep SUSTAIN_LIMIT = 5 min

**Recommendation: keep 5 minutes.**

**For an outage that never lets up, both settings are bounded.**
- 5 min pages at the first failure 5 min into the run: 08:30:50 in N10's timeline, 09:25:50 in a pre-open variant (blip at 09:00, outage from 09:20).
- 2 min pages at the same instant the T-2 open rule fires: 08:27:20 and 09:22:20.
- So 5 min costs at most about 3.5 extra minutes, and only for a second outage within 30 min of an announced pair.

**The hold is not silent.**
- In session, the hold forwards the child exits and the bridge's own CRITICAL lines.
- Pre-open, the half-hour digest carried "⚡ IB Gateway flapped 1× since 08:27 ET" at 08:27:21 in the probe.

**The 2-minute limit turns the pair budget into a near no-op.** It would then hold back only bursts shorter than 2 minutes. That brings back the pair-per-blip noise the owner rejected in 904af38 F-1. In my BrokerWatch simulation (scratch `probes/sustain_compare.py`):

| Scenario | 2 min | 5 min |
|---|---|---|
| Five 4-minute port flaps | 6 alert/recovery pairs | 2 pairs and a digest flap count |
| Six 3-minute IBKR losses (the bridge's own 1100 CRITICAL line still goes out each time under either setting) | 7 pairs | 2 pairs |
| Four 5.5-minute IBKR losses | 5 pairs | 2 pairs |

**If the owner's rule is strictly "never late", 2 min is the faster choice.** Its noise is limited to outages of 2–5 min (2–6 min for 1100 losses). A narrower option for T-4 is 2 min only before 09:25 ET, when no bridge line can speak.
